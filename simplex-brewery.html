The Simplex Method, Step by Step

A craft brewery maximizes weekly contribution margin - watch the tableau pivot and the corner point walk the feasible region.

One simplex iteration

  1. Entering variable. Pick the most negative coefficient in the z-row; that variable enters the basis. If no coefficient is negative, the current solution is optimal and we stop.
  2. Leaving variable. Minimum ratio test: divide each RHS by the positive entry in the entering column. The row with the smallest ratio is the pivot row, its basic variable leaves, and the entry where pivot row and entering column meet is the pivot element.
  3. Pivot. Divide the pivot row by the pivot element, then add or subtract multiples of it from every other row, including the z-row, until the entering column is a unit column. Back to step 1.
